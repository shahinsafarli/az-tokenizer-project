{\Large\bfseries Team Contribution Report}\\[3pt]
{\large Cross-lingual Transfer between Azerbaijani and Turkish}\\[3pt]
\texttt{github.com/shahinsafarli/az-tokenizer-project}

\small
The project was divided into five balanced technical work packages. Through the
code, configuration, testing, and evidence phase (PR01--PR20), every member owns
four repository commits. Each member contributed to both implementation and a
separate validation or evidence area. The final submission artifacts were
reviewed jointly by the team.

\vspace{5pt}
\textbf{Shared finalization.} The team jointly checked the final report,
presentation, contribution statement, repository structure, source archive,
results archive, and release readiness. Report claims are backed by committed
aggregate results and the SHA-256-verified archive containing 164 run records and
164 logs.

\textbf{Verification record.} The complete non-slow test suite passed with 90
tests and one slow test deselected. Configuration hashes, result JSON/CSV files,
figures, split manifests, archive membership, and the 164-entry held-out-test
ledger were checked before submission.

\textbf{Contribution declaration.} The percentages summarize the agreed overall
division of project work. All team members reviewed and accept responsibility
for the submitted code, experimental evidence, paper, and presentation.
